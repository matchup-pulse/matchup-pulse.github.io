❓ Where do I find my ID? (Click to expand)
  • Open your team page on fantasy.espn.com in a web browser.
  • Look at the website address bar—your URL contains leagueId=12345678 and teamId=4.
  • Copy that entire link and paste it into the Quick Paste box above to auto-fill both numbers!
🔓 League Private? How to make it viewable to public
  1. Have your League Manager (LM) open the league on fantasy.espn.com (desktop or mobile browser—not the app).
  2. Go to LM Tools → League Settings → Basic Settings.
  3. Set Make League Viewable to Public to Yes and click Save Changes.
